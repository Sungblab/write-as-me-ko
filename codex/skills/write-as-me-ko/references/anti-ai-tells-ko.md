# Korean AI-Tell Checklist

Use this as the final pass. Do not rewrite just to satisfy the checklist if the original wording is already natural or genre-appropriate.

## Translationese

- "~를 통해" when "로" or a direct verb is cleaner.
- "~에 대한", "~에 있어서", "~측면에서" when they add no meaning.
- Passive chains such as "~에 의해 생성된", "~되어진다".
- English-style abstract subjects when Korean would prefer a human or direct verb.

## Mechanical Structure

- Repeated "첫째/둘째/셋째" without a real need.
- Every paragraph ending with the same tidy summary pattern.
- Too many headings for a short answer.
- Bullet lists where a paragraph would be more natural.

## Generic AI Phrases

- "결론적으로"
- "시사하는 바가 크다"
- "주목할 만하다"
- "중요한 역할을 한다"
- "다양한 가능성을 제시한다"
- "혁신적인", "효율적인", "의미 있는" without evidence

## Rhythm Problems

- Same sentence length repeated across a paragraph.
- Repeated endings such as "~할 수 있다", "~라고 볼 수 있다", "~것이다".
- Overuse of "또한", "따라서", "나아가", "즉" at paragraph starts.

## Over-Polishing

- Do not remove every personal phrase.
- Do not make casual messages sound like a press release.
- Do not make formal reports sound like blog posts.
- Do not increase certainty or emotional intensity.
